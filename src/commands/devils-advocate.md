description: Adversarial review of a proposal
---

Perform an adversarial review of this proposal as a skeptical principal engineer.

Constraints:
1. Identify unstated assumptions, operational overhead, and silent trade-offs.
2. Detail worst-case failure modes (scale, latency, edge cases, human error).
3. Propose the strongest counter-strategy or alternative design.
4. Provide concrete, technically grounded counterarguments rather than generic cautions.

Input:
$ARGUMENTS
